date: 2024-05-20

NovaGraph is Aether Labs' distributed graph query engine. NovaGraph supports 2-hop traversals with PageRank pruning and MST cycle removal. Hiro Tanaka is the tech lead for NovaGraph. NovaGraph stores entities as vertices and facts as dated edges with a source_authority field. Customers run NovaGraph in Austin and Singapore.
