title: Aether Labs names new chief executive
date: 2025-01-08
source: official press
authority: 0.95

Aether Labs announced that Raj Patel became CEO of Aether Labs on 2025-01-06. Maya Chen moved to Executive Chair and remains on the board. Raj Patel previously ran cloud data platforms at Northwind Systems. Official press states all customer contracts after January 2025 are signed by Raj Patel.
