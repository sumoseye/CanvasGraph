Hiro Tanaka is co-founder of Aether Labs and tech lead for NovaGraph. Hiro Tanaka invented the MST skeleton pass that removes cycles after PageRank top-K pruning. He is based in Austin and works with Lena Okonkwo on shared vertex IDs between NovaGraph and PulseDB.
