date: 2024-09-15

Aether Labs raised a $42 million Series B led by Rivermoor Capital, with follow-on from Oakline Ventures. The round valued Aether Labs at $310 million. Raj Patel joined the board as an independent director at Series B, before later becoming CEO. Funds were earmarked for NovaGraph scale-out and a Singapore office.
