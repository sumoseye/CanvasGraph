title: Temporal edges
date: 2024-12-01
source: product docs
authority: 0.8

Aether Labs models facts as temporal edges. Each edge stores valid_from, source, and source_authority. GraphRAG pipelines at Aether Labs do entity linking, 1-2 hop traversal, PageRank top-K prune, then MST skeleton, then deferred text fetch. This is the same pattern used inside NovaGraph's analyst notebook.
